Live demo · Article Scraper

Pick a topic. Get today’s news, scraped live.

Choose a category and this runs the scraper’s pipeline in your browser against that category’s real RSS feeds: fetch, parse, clean, score each article with the same keywords and weights as the Dart code, dedupe, then append the results to a sheet you can download. Every row links to the original article.

Fetch

Some feeds (ESPN, Wired, NASA, the New York Times, CNBC) let browsers read them directly. Browsers block cross-site reads of the rest, so they go through rss2json (10 newest items per feed), with allorigins as a fallback. The real CLI fetches every feed directly.

Score

Each category has its own keywords and topic groups; the weights are shared. Articles score for keyword matches, headline matches, topic coverage and density, plus priority tickers for finance, the same as content_analyzer.dart. Categories live in categories.dart.

Export

Rows are appended in batches with the same columns as the CLI’s --xlsx and Google Sheets export. Download the result as .xlsx or .csv. Each article has a clickable link.
